2. Executive Summary

Stripe is the world's preeminent financial infrastructure platform, processing over \$1.4T in annual payment volume for millions of companies ranging from early-stage startups to Fortune 500 giants like Amazon, Microsoft, and Uber. Headquartered dual-regionally in South San Francisco and Dublin, Ireland, Stripe has evolved from a 7-line developer payments API into a full-stack financial operating system comprising Billing, Connect, Tax, Radar (Fraud), and Treasury.

Financially resilient, cash-flow positive, and generating an estimated \$4.5B+ in net revenue, Stripe's recent strategic focus centers on AI agentic commerce APIs, enterprise global acquiring, and automated back-office revenue operations.

4. Business Model & Monetization Architecture

How They Make Money

•
Transaction Processing Fees: Core engine charging 2.9% + \$0.30 per successful card charge in the US (blended lower volume rates for enterprises ~1.5%–2.1%).
•
SaaS Software Subscriptions: Stripe Billing (0.5%–0.8% of recurring volume), Stripe Tax (0.5% per transaction), Stripe Radar for Teams (\$0.02/tx).
•
Platform Take Rates (Stripe Connect): Fee splits on marketplace transactions plus \$2/month per active custom connected account.
•
Treasury & FX Spreads: 1%–2% currency conversion markups on cross-border transactions and interest float on custodial balances.
