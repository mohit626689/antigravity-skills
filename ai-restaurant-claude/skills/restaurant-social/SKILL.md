## The 7-Theme Weekly Framework

Every restaurant content calendar should rotate through these themes:

| Day | Theme | Why It Works |
|-----|-------|--------------|
| Monday | **Menu Spotlight / New Dish** | Drives orders, helps new menu items |
| Tuesday | **Behind the Scenes** | Builds connection — kitchen, prep, ingredient sourcing |
| Wednesday | **Staff Feature** | Humanizes brand — server stories, chef intros |
| Thursday | **Customer Feature / UGC** | Social proof — repost guest photos |
| Friday | **Weekend Push** | Drive Friday/Saturday reservations & specials |
| Saturday | **High-Energy Atmosphere** | Live music, packed crowd, weekend vibe |
| Sunday | **Family / Slow / Story** | Brunch, history, owner story, traditions |

## Platform-Specific Rules

### Instagram

- **Feed posts:** 4-7 per week
- **Stories:** Daily (3-5 per day)
- **Reels:** 2-3 per week (Reels = #1 reach driver in 2026)
- **Carousels:** 1-2 per week (highest engagement)
- **Best post times:** 11am-1pm (lunch decision time), 4-6pm (dinner decision time), 8-10pm (next-day planning)
- **Caption length:** First 125 chars are everything (the "hook" before "more")
- **Hashtags:** 5-10 mixed (local + cuisine + niche), placed at end of caption

### TikTok

- **Posts:** 3-5 per week (more is better — algorithm rewards frequency)
- **Format:** 15-60 seconds, vertical 9:16
- **Hooks:** First 1.5 seconds determines watch-through
- **Best content types:** Recipes / techniques, "POV" guest experience, kitchen reveals, staff personality
- **Music:** Trending sounds (check For You Page weekly)
- **Best post times:** 6-10am, 7-11pm

### Facebook

- **Posts:** 3-5 per week
- **Best content types:** Events, longer-form stories, photo albums
- **Audience skew:** Older / family-focused — content should match
- **Best post times:** 9am, 1pm, 7pm

## Reels Idea Bank (Use Anywhere)

1. **"3 Things You Didn't Know About [Dish]"** — Quick text-overlay reel
2. **Order, Open, Taste** — POV of takeout being received
3. **"Make This at Home"** — Quick recipe with brand mention at end
4. **Day in the Life of [Chef]** — 60s montage
5. **Plating Speed-Run** — 15s of plating signature dish
6. **Customer Reaction** — Captured (with permission) first bite reactions
7. **Behind the Apron** — Chef talks about ingredient sourcing
8. **Friday Night Energy** — 15s montage of packed dining room
9. **Wine Pairing Tips** — Sommelier or owner talks pairings
10. **Family Recipe Story** — Owner tells the recipe's origin

## TikTok Idea Bank

1. **"Things I'd Order If I Were You"** — Trending POV format
2. **"Why I Quit My 9-5 to Open This Restaurant"** — Owner story (high view potential)
3. **"Rating Italian Dishes (As an Italian)"** — Chef rates iconic dishes
4. **"Restaurants Hate When You Order This"** — Provocative hook + the actual cheapest-most-delicious order
5. **"What $20 Gets You at My Restaurant"** — Price-value content
6. **"How We Actually Make [Signature]"** — Process video
7. **Trending Sound + Restaurant** — Match trending audio to relevant content
8. **"Customer Asked Me What's Good — Here's the Honest Answer"** — Server POV
9. **"Stitch Reply" to a food critic** — Engagement strategy
10. **"Day Before Service Starts"** — Empty restaurant being prepped
